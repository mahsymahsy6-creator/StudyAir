# P11 Execution Report: Streak Calibration & Exam Study Plans

## 1. Summary
Implemented streak calibration to count completed study blocks towards maintaining and advancing study streaks, and added automated exam study plan generation:
1. **Streak engine calibration**:
   - `deriveStudyDay` now counts `blocksCompleted >= 1` as qualifying to earn the study day alongside tasks and focus minutes.
   - Preserves free days (weekends and designated holidays without classes) so students are not penalized when there is no school.
   - Connected `studyBlocks` state directly to `calculateStreak` in `useMobileStudyStore()`.
2. **"Buat rencana belajar" for upcoming exams**:
   - `createExamStudyPlan()` generates structured review (45m) and practice (60m) tasks with custom deadlines matching the exam date.
   - Integrated into `HomeScreen` with upcoming exam countdown card and one-tap "Buat rencana belajar" triggering automatic creation and schedule sheet preview.
